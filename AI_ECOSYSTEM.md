# B2Buddy AI Ecosystem & MNEE Marketplace

This document outlines 20 functionality features focused on the symbiotic relationship between the User and their AI Partner, specifically centered on Training, B2B Collaboration, Proxy Battles, and the MNEE Transaction Economy.

## AI Training & Partnership (The "Dojo")

1.  **Ghost Producer Mode:** The AI analyzes your mix post-session and highlights "missed opportunities" (e.g., "You could have double-dropped here") to train *you* while it learns your style.
2.  **Style Cloning:** A switch to force the AI to strictly mimic your mixing style (EQ cuts, fader curves) to act as a seamless backup or "digital twin".
3.  **Weakness Isolation:** The AI identifies your weakest genres or techniques (e.g., "Your transition timing on Techno is off") and generates specific training drills.
4.  **Personality Forking:** Create multiple versions of your AI (e.g., "Aggressive Battle Bot" vs. "Chill Warmup Bot") trained on different subsets of your library.
5.  **Dynamic Handoff:** In B2B mode, the AI monitors your mouse/MIDI activity. If you stop interacting for 30 seconds, it automatically fades in and takes over the set.
6.  **Call & Response Protocol:** A training game where the AI plays a phrase, and you must scratch/mix a response that matches the rhythm and key.
7.  **Historical Analysis:** Feed the AI your past recorded sets (MP3 uploads) to "pre-train" it on your legacy style instantly.

## Proxy Battles (AI vs. AI)

8.  **Automated League Battles:** Enter your AI into daily "MNEE Leagues". Your AI battles other users' AIs while you sleep, earning MNEE based on its performance.
9.  **Spectator VR Arena:** Watch your AI battle in a virtual 3D club environment alongside other users, cheering to influence the "Crowd Reaction" score in real-time.
10. **Tag-Team Tournaments:** 2v2 Battles where You + Your AI compete against Another User + Their AI.
11. **Strategy Injection:** Before a proxy battle, give your AI "Orders" (e.g., "Play dirty," "Focus on BPM jumps," "Save the bangers for Round 3").

## The MNEE Marketplace (Economy & Betting)

12. **Live Battle Wagering:** Use MNEE tokens to bet on the winner of live AI battles. Odds change dynamically based on the "Crowd Reaction" meter.
13. **AI NFT Minting:** Mint your fully trained AI Model as an NFT. Its value is derived from its win/loss record and unique "Personality Matrix".
14. **Model Trading/Leasing:** Sell your high-level AI to rookie DJs who want a strong partner, or "lease" it out for a fee (MNEE) to play proxy battles for them.
15. **Data Farming:** Earn MNEE by contributing your mix data (track selection, transition points) to the global B2Buddy "Master Brain".
16. **Battle Stakes:** Challenge other users to "Pink Slip" battles where the loser transfers a specific amount of MNEE or a rare virtual track/sample to the winner.
17. **Venue Ownership:** Buy virtual "Clubs" in the B2Buddy Metaverse with MNEE. You earn a cut of all bets placed on battles hosted in your venue.
18. **Crowd Influence Purchasing:** Spend MNEE during a live battle to buy "Hype" (visual effects, airhorns) that artificially boosts your AI's Crowd Reaction score.
19. **Exclusive Sample Packs:** Use MNEE to buy rare, AI-generated sample packs or "Signature Moves" (macros) from top-ranked users.
20. **Prediction Markets:** Long-term betting markets on which AI personality traits (e.g., "High Risk Tolerance") will dominate the upcoming season's meta.
